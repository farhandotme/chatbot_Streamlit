Cricket is one of the most popular sports in the world.
It is played and loved by millions of people across different countries.
The game originated in England.
Over time, it spread to many parts of the world.
Today, cricket is especially popular in countries like India, Australia, England, and Pakistan.
It is played in different formats.
The main formats are Test cricket, One Day Internationals, and T20 cricket.
Each format has its own excitement and strategy.
Cricket is played between two teams.
Each team has eleven players.
The game is played on a circular or oval ground.
At the center of the ground is a rectangular pitch.
The pitch is very important in cricket.
The match begins with a toss.
The captain who wins the toss chooses to bat or bowl.
Batting means scoring runs.
Bowling means trying to get the batsman out.
The team that bats first tries to score as many runs as possible.
The other team tries to stop them.
A batsman uses a bat to hit the ball.
A bowler throws the ball towards the batsman.
Fielders try to catch or stop the ball.
Runs are scored in different ways.
The batsman can run between the wickets.
They can also hit boundaries.
A four is scored when the ball touches the ground and crosses the boundary.
A six is scored when the ball goes over the boundary without touching the ground.
Getting a batsman out is called a wicket.
There are many ways to get out.
Bowled is when the ball hits the stumps.
Caught is when a fielder catches the ball.
Run out happens when the batsman fails to reach the crease.
LBW stands for Leg Before Wicket.
Cricket requires skill and patience.
Test matches are the longest format.
They are played over five days.
Each team gets two innings.
Test cricket is considered the purest form.
It tests endurance and technique.
One Day Internationals are shorter.
Each team gets 50 overs.
An over consists of six balls.
T20 cricket is the shortest format.
Each team gets 20 overs.
It is fast and exciting.
T20 is very popular among young fans.
